# 英文白癡也能用的產品與問答句型

你的目標不是自由發揮，而是熟練少量「積木句」。所有上台稿都由這些句型組成。

## 十二個必背句型

### 1. 產品是什麼

> **[Product] is a tool that helps [user] [verb].**

例：

> EDT HMI Studio is a tool that helps teams build embedded touch interfaces.

### 2. 使用者可以做什麼

> **Users can [verb A], [verb B], and [verb C].**

> Users can design the screens, connect device values, and test the result.

### 3. 痛點

> **Today, [work] is often [problem].**

> Today, HMI development is often split across several tools.

### 4. 解法

> **We bring [A], [B], and [C] into one workflow.**

### 5. 主要好處

> **The main benefit is [noun].**

> The main benefit is earlier validation.

### 6. 不用做什麼

> **Instead of [old way], users can [new way].**

> Instead of writing every interaction in C, users can define it visually.

### 7. 目前支援

> **The current build supports [scope].**

不要只說 `We support...`，加 `current build` 比較不會把 roadmap 說成現況。

### 8. 有條件

> **For supported [boards / workflows / protocols], users can...**

### 9. 路線圖

> **It is on our roadmap, but it is not part of the current release scope.**

### 10. 不知道

> **I do not want to give you an unofficial answer. Let me confirm that and follow up.**

### 11. 確認問題

> **If I understand your question correctly, you are asking about [topic]. Is that right?**

### 12. 收尾

> **The best next step is to validate your board, protocol, and target use case together.**

## 問答只用 A-B-C

每個答案最多三段：

1. **A — Answer:** 先直接回答 yes / no / currently / it depends。
2. **B — Benefit or boundary:** 說價值或限制。
3. **C — Check:** 反問需求或提出下一步。

例：對方問 `Does it support CAN?`

> **A:** Not as a production path in the current build.  
> **B:** The data model and board definitions are prepared for CAN, but the runtime stack is still roadmap work.  
> **C:** Is raw CAN, CANopen, or J1939 the requirement for your target customer?

這比講五分鐘 CAN 架構更像高階主管的回答。

## 聽不懂時的救命句

### 請對方重說

> Sorry, could you say that once more, a little more slowly?

### 只聽不懂一個字

> I understood the main question, but I missed the last term. Could you repeat that part?

### 確認自己的理解

> Let me make sure I understood. Are you asking about the commercial model or the technical integration?

### 問題太大

> There are two parts to that question. Let me take the technical part first.

### 需要工程同事

> I can give you the product-level answer now. For the board-level detail, I would like our engineer to confirm the exact configuration.

### 需要查公司政策

> That depends on our approved commercial policy. I will confirm it and send you a written answer.

## 爭取三秒思考時間

不要發出長長的 `uh...`。使用完整短句：

- `That is a good question.`
- `The short answer is yes, with one condition.`
- `Let me answer that in two parts.`
- `From the product side...`
- `For the current release...`

說完後停一秒是專業，不是失敗。

## 簡單連接詞

只需要這六個：

- **First** — 第一點
- **Second** — 第二點
- **For example** — 例如
- **The key point is** — 重點是
- **However** — 但是
- **So** — 因此

不要堆 `moreover`, `furthermore`, `nevertheless`, `consequently`。它們不會讓產品更好懂。

## 把難英文換成簡單英文

| 難或危險 | 簡單版本 |
|---|---|
| utilize | use |
| facilitate | help |
| approximately | about |
| numerous | many |
| seamless | one workflow / fewer handoffs |
| revolutionary | new / different |
| comprehensive | covers A, B, and C |
| compatible with everything | supports these specific targets |
| guarantee | designed to / intended to |
| soon | currently planned for [date] |

## 最小回答模板

任何問題腦袋空白時：

> The short answer is **[yes / no / currently / it depends]**.  
> For the current release, **[one fact or limitation]**.  
> The best next step is **[validation or follow-up]**.

這三句先練到不用想。流利不是講很多，而是任何時候都能找到下一句。
