# 你應該反問對方的探索問題

高階會議不是口試。你問得好，對方才會把真正需求告訴你，也能避免產品被錯誤配對。

## 開始問之前

> To make the discussion relevant, may I ask a few questions about your target market and first use case?

## 問總經理或事業主管

1. > What business outcome would make this product valuable for your organization?
2. > Is your priority software revenue, hardware pull-through, faster customer evaluation, or lower support cost?
3. > Which customer segment would you target first?
4. > What problem do those customers struggle with today?
5. > How do customers currently build and maintain their HMI applications?
6. > Where does the current process lose the most time?
7. > Who owns the buying decision: engineering, product management, purchasing, or the business unit?
8. > What evidence would you need before approving a regional launch?
9. > What would make you decide not to proceed?
10. > If a pilot succeeds, what is the likely next decision?

## 問業務副總或區域主管

1. > How many relevant accounts does your team actively cover?
2. > Which industries have the strongest demand for embedded touch interfaces?
3. > Are these mainly new designs or replacements for existing products?
4. > What are the common display sizes, MCUs, and protocols in your pipeline?
5. > At what stage do customers normally ask for software support?
6. > Which competitor or current workflow do customers use today?
7. > What is the typical proof-of-concept cycle?
8. > What usually blocks conversion after a successful demo?
9. > Which account could be a realistic first reference project?
10. > Who on your team should own the next customer qualification?

## 問通路商總經理

1. > What investment would your team need to make before selling this product?
2. > What protection or commercial clarity do you need in return?
3. > How is your pre-sales team structured?
4. > Can your team support first-line board and protocol qualification?
5. > Which local languages are required for sales, training, and support?
6. > How do you register and protect opportunities with other vendors today?
7. > What margin structure is necessary for your business model?
8. > Do you prefer stand-alone software, a hardware bundle, or a service-led offer?
9. > What support issues would you expect to escalate to EDT?
10. > What three outcomes should we measure in the first six months?

## 問技術主管

1. > What is the target MCU and board?
2. > What are the display resolution, color format, and orientation?
3. > Which touch controller and display interface are used?
4. > Which physical connector carries the device communication?
5. > Which protocol does the device speak?
6. > Does the panel initiate communication, or does the host drive the panel?
7. > How many values are exchanged, and how often must they update?
8. > Are the values bits, integers, floats, text, or a mixture?
9. > Are there any custom frames, login sequences, checksums, or binary payloads?
10. > What memory and flash constraints matter?
11. > Is multilingual text required? Which languages and fonts?
12. > Are images, video, or animations required?
13. > What is the current build and flashing toolchain?
14. > What must be proven in the Emulator, and what must be proven on hardware?
15. > What are the acceptance tests for the proof of concept?

## 問產品經理

1. > How many screens and user flows are in the first release?
2. > Which interactions are standard, and which are product-specific?
3. > How often does the UI change after engineering starts?
4. > Who owns text, translation, and visual approval?
5. > Which parts of the UI should be reusable across product models?
6. > What is the expected product lifetime and update process?
7. > Which field issues are hardest to reproduce today?
8. > What information should a service engineer see in diagnostics?
9. > Which feature is essential for launch, and which can wait?
10. > How will you define a successful user experience?

## 四個必要資格問題

時間不夠時，只問這四個：

> Which board are you targeting?  
> Which protocol does the device use?  
> Who starts the communication?  
> What must the proof of concept demonstrate?

## 追問句型

- `Could you give me one example?`
- `How does that work today?`
- `How often does that happen?`
- `Who owns that decision?`
- `What is the impact when it fails?`
- `Which part is mandatory for the first release?`
- `What would be an acceptable workaround?`

## 確認你聽懂

> Let me summarize. Your first target is an 800-by-480 HMI, the host drives the panel through Modbus RTU, and the proof of concept must show five screens, Traditional Chinese, and a successful hardware flash. Did I capture that correctly?

這句會讓你的英文聽起來比背很多單字更專業。
