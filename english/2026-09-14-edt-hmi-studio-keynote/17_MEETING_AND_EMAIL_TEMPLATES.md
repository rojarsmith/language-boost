# 會議開場、轉場、收尾與會後 Email

## 第一次見面

> Good morning, Mr. / Ms. **[Name]**. I am **[Name]**, **[title]** at **[company]**. Thank you for making time for this discussion.

若不確定稱謂，直接用名字：

> Good morning, Alex. It is a pleasure to meet you.

## 確認時間

> We have forty-five minutes. I suggest a five-minute introduction, a short product demo, and then a discussion of your market and questions. Does that work for you?

## 取得開始同意

> Before I begin, is there one topic you especially want us to cover today?

## 簡報轉 Demo

> Rather than list more features, let me show one complete workflow.

## Demo 轉 Q&A

> That completes the end-to-end path. I would now like to open the discussion and understand where this may fit your market.

## 把問題交給同事

> I will give the product-level answer first, and then I would like **[Name]** to add the board-level detail.

不要只說 `Maybe John can answer.`，那像把問題丟掉。

## 時間快到了

> We have about ten minutes left. I suggest we focus on the two decisions needed for a next step: the pilot scope and the commercial owner.

## 暫停一個離題問題

> That topic is important, but it is outside today's product scope. May I put it in the follow-up list and return to the pilot decision?

## 口頭總結

> Let me summarize what I heard. Your first target is **[customer/use case]**, using **[board]** and **[protocol]**. **[Name]** will provide **[input]** by **[date]**, and we will return a proof-of-concept proposal by **[date]**. I also owe you written answers on **[open items]**.
