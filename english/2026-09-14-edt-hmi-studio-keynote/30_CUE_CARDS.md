# 12 keynote cue cards

Print one card per half page or copy each card to a presenter-note page. Look at the card only between sections. The bold line is the sentence you must say exactly.

## Card 1: Welcome and moment of truth

Opening lines:

> Hello, everyone. //
>
> Welcome to everyone here in the room. /
>
> And a warm welcome to our colleagues and partners / joining us online from around the world. //
>
> I'm very happy to have this opportunity / to introduce this exciting new product to you. //
>
> Thank you all for joining us today. //

Then begin the product story:

**Every embedded product has a moment of truth.**

- Customer touches the screen
- Clear, fast, trustworthy
- Simple screen, complicated work
- Two chapters
- US$200M is a North Star and scenario, not approved guidance

Stage:

- Stand still
- Smile and greet the room
- Look briefly toward the camera when welcoming online colleagues and partners
- Pause after “today,” then return to the room for the product story
- Pause after “one thing”

## Card 2: Old workflow

**What if the whole HMI could live in one project?**

- Designer makes picture
- Engineer rebuilds in code
- Protocol and hardware arrive later
- Late problem means more time and risk
- Sales and FAE also wait

Stage:

- Point to the workflow once
- Slow down before the question

## Card 3: Product reveal

**This is EDT HMI Studio.**

- Visual development for embedded touch interfaces
- Design, logic, communication, emulation, build, flash
- Supported workflows can reach firmware without application code
- Engineers keep control

Stage:

- Black screen first
- Say the name
- Two-second pause

## Card 4: AI collaborator

**The assistant uses the editor's own controls.**

- Create project
- Visual reference to screens
- Focused edits
- Normal editable content
- One undo step
- Never operates target hardware

Risk:

- Never promise perfect results
- OpenRouter is external
- Ollama is local only with a local host

## Card 5: Live edit

**Let me show you one small change.**

Prompt:

> Make the main action button green. Keep the text and the layout unchanged.

Sequence:

- Submit
- Silence
- Apply
- Inspect
- Undo

Fallback:

> I have the completed result ready. Let me show you the same change without making you wait.

## Card 6: Engineering path

**AI gives us speed. The rest of Studio gives us an engineering path.**

- Design
- Logic
- Protocol
- Real-LVGL Emulator
- Customer Demo
- Supported build and flash

Proof:

- Simulated value changes HMI
- Touch sends a write

## Card 7: Current scope

**Let me be precise about the current scope.**

- Use approved Alpha or Beta line
- Public evaluation build
- Three board profiles
- Modbus RTU and serial commands
- Board capabilities differ
- CAN and Ethernet outside current production path

Stage:

- Calm tone
- No apology
- Boundaries create trust

## Card 8: Design-in flywheel

**Studio can change when EDT enters the customer conversation.**

- Customer idea
- Working interface
- EDT board profile
- Production display
- Repeat orders
- Variant and next generation

Proof needed:

- Evaluation to design-in conversion
- Program value and retention

## Card 9: Three engines

**The model has three revenue engines.**

1. Production display programs
2. AI and enterprise services
3. Strategic OEM and ecosystem agreements

Numbers:

- 80 × US$2M = US$160M
- 2,000 × US$10K = US$20M
- 10 × US$2M = US$20M

## Card 10: US$200M reveal

**Here is the North Star.**

**A repeatable US$200 million annual revenue engine.**

Immediately:

> This is a scenario model. It is not approved guidance.

Then:

- Not a promise
- Not software ARR
- Every line can be tested
- Total annual run-rate: US$200M

Stage:

- Three-second silence on the number
- Say every unit
- Never attach an unapproved year

## Card 11: Validation

**The assumptions give us a management system.**

Five gates:

1. Demand
2. Conversion
3. Economics
4. Repeatability
5. Scale

First 90 days:

- Focused accounts
- Controlled evaluations
- Track board validation
- Test one paid offer
- Review evidence

## Card 12: Closing

**EDT already knows how to build the display.**

- Customer idea becomes visible
- Project connects interface, data, and hardware
- EDT competes for production
- Next product begins from trusted work
- Scenario remains unapproved until evidence supports it

Final lines:

> EDT HMI Studio helps us win the work around that display and carry it into the next product.
>
> That is the opportunity.
>
> Thank you.

Stage:

- Center
- Look across the room
- Slow final three lines
- Hold two seconds

## Q&A card

Current fact:

> Today, the current build supports...

Boundary:

> For this board, provider, or release...

Next proof:

> The next step is to validate...

Unknown:

> I do not want to give you an unofficial answer. I will confirm the approved position with [owner] and follow up by [date].

Missed word:

> I understood the main question, but I missed the last term. Could you repeat that part?

Financial correction:

> Let me correct one number. The scenario is [number and unit]. It is an assumption, not approved guidance.
