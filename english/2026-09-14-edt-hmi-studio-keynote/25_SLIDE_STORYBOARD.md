# 23-slide keynote storyboard

This is a content and visual brief, not a finished deck. It matches [the 30-minute script](23_30_MINUTE_KEYNOTE_SCRIPT.md) slide for slide.

## Visual direction

- 16:9 widescreen
- Black or near-black background
- White type with EDT green used only for focus
- One composition per slide
- Minimal copy, generous empty space
- Product screenshots at native aspect ratio
- Large titles: at least 42 pt
- Slide titles: at least 32 pt
- Body copy: at least 20 pt when shown
- No card grids, fake app controls, decorative dashboards, or dense feature lists
- Use EDT logo assets without distortion
- Use launch-style pacing through silence and progressive reveals, without copying Apple wording or trademarks

## Build notes by slide

### Slides 1 to 3

- Slide 1 contains no agenda and no presenter biography.
- Slide 2 uses plain words. Keep the loop visually obvious.
- Slide 3 remains on screen for the first product definition. Avoid extra taglines.

### Slides 4 to 7

- Use the latest source screenshots. Do not use mock UI.
- Crop images to protect readable product detail.
- Slide 6 must match the exact live Demo or recorded fallback.
- Put AI limits in speaker notes and say them aloud. Do not crowd the slide.

### Slides 8 to 12

- Use one screenshot per slide.
- Slide 10 must say Modbus RTU and serial commands. Do not add CAN or Ethernet logos.
- Slide 11 uses the Emulator or Customer Demo live when reliable.
- Slide 12 must be refreshed within 24 hours of the event. Replace Alpha with Beta only after the official page changes and the claim is approved.

### Slides 13 to 15

- Slide 13 is a visual breath. Keep it on screen for less than 30 seconds.
- Slide 14 is the only flywheel diagram.
- Slide 15 explains operating roles. It does not make a financial claim.

### Slides 16 to 20

- Every number carries a unit and annual basis.
- Label Slides 16 to 19 with “Illustrative scenario” in a visible footer.
- On Slide 19, show the qualification no later than the second spoken sentence.
- Slide 20 source notes:
  - Grand View Research, Human Machine Interface Market, current page accessed 2026-09-18.
  - Calculation: US$200M ÷ US$11.60B = 1.72%.
- Do not place the AI-in-manufacturing market number on a slide unless Marketing and IR approve its relevance. It may stay in speaker notes.

### Slides 21 to 23

- Slide 21 shows the gates, not a detailed project plan.
- Slide 22 should name actual owners after management assigns them.
- Slide 23 removes every number and returns attention to the product and speaker.

## Do not place on slides before approval

- “EDT will reach US$200M”
- A year attached to the US$200M target
- Revenue CAGR, margin, earnings, or valuation claims
- Customer logos, pipeline counts, or design-win claims
- “Free software” as a final commercial policy
- Beta or GA wording that is newer than the official page
- Security, privacy, SLA, or regional exclusivity promises
